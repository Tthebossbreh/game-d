LEVEL DESIGN
- Level 1
- - Base movement
- - Getting player used to controls
- - Basic obstacles
- - Fish increase your score
- - Speedrun bonus time 50% fish completion 50%
- - A slightly darker version of the base background/props

- Level 2
- - Moving/timed obstacles
- - Halfway through level two you get a dash
- - Tutorial section for dash
- - Fast (short?) sequence/chase
- - Bigger fish chases you
- - Bigger obstacles (anemones) sway in a timed pattern
- - Moray eels that pop out on a pattern
- - Starfish
- - Urchin

- Level 3
- - Underwater cave
- - Two routes
- - 1 route difficult and short (purple)
- - 2nd route easy but long (blue)
- - Crystals
- - Breakable crystal platforms
- - More aggressive crystal fish that chases you

- Level 4
- - Volcano foreshadowing -> lvl4 route to get there
- - Horizontal movement focus / momentum?
- - Fish spawn randomly
- - Open, sandy area. maybe fast streams to encourage faster horizontal movement?
- - Ends going into a darker, rockier area
- - Streams: pull you down/in different directions
- - Turtle obstacles

- Level 5
- - Medium difficulty platforming through a cave with volcanic hints
- - Boss foreshadowing
- - Kelp dragon boss fight
- - Escape from volcano


Small loop: individual levels/runs
Big loop: replaying to get a better score (all 5 levels)

The first 10 minutes will likely be the player figuring out the controls, score system, and health system in level 1. If the player has experience with platformers, the 10 minutes will likely be spent going through the levels for the first time, not doing optimization for speed yet. The levels will have several rooms with increasingly challenging obstacles.
