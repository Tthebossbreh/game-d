* Diver 
* Platformer
* Implement gravity
* 1 level
* 3 damage health system

LEVEL DESIGN
- Level 1
- - Base movement
- - Getting player used to controls
- - Basic obstacles
- - Fish increase your score
- - Score multiplier by times, starts are 3.0x and scales down to 1.0x
- - Uses base background/props
- - The level loops once you finish it, restarting only when you lose all 3 hearts (vigor)

Small game loop: Each level completion
Big game loop: losing all 3 vigor and having to reset
Even bigger: trying to get a highscore

10 minute gameplay: 
The player would likely spend several minutes on their first run, just trying to get to the end and possibly getting some of the fish. Some of the obstacles they would learn to get around after taking damage to them, and eventually learn to avoid properly. After several attempts like this, some time would be spent figuring out how to get as many fish as possible each run and more consistent movement. Eventually, runs would aim for high scores, trying to get all the fish and finish the level both as many times as possible and as quickly as possible.
