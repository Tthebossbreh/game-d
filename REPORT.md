Playtest 1: Our first playtest was with the level editor, first making sure it worked and adding certain features to it like a stamp tool. We began to create the first level with it. We also implemented proper gravity rather than swimming to make sure that it was actually a platformer. After we implemented gravity, we tested the platforming in the level. We mostly did these tests to get a feel for the route through the level, and the jumping for the character.

Playtest 2: Our second playtest continued to improve the level editor by adding an entity remove feature, which allowed us to delete and move the exit, fish, mines, etc. We continued working on the level format and the route the player would take through the level. After the map was finish, collision was added. The rest of the playtesting consisted of making sure the score and health saved, making sure the fish and mines reset, and other small details to make sure everything was working as well as practicing the gameplay in general.
